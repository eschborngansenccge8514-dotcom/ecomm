# LHDN e-Invoice Implementation Phases

## Implementation Timeline
The Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (LHDN) is implementing e-invoicing in three phases based on annual turnover or revenue:

- **Phase 1: 1 August 2024**
  - Mandatory for taxpayers with annual turnover or revenue of **RM100 million and above**.
- **Phase 2: 1 January 2025**
  - Mandatory for taxpayers with annual turnover or revenue between **RM25 million and RM100 million**.
- **Phase 3: 1 July 2025**
  - Mandatory for **all other taxpayers**, regardless of turnover size.

## Determining Annual Turnover
Taxpayers should refer to their Audited Financial Statements for the financial year 2022 or 2023 to determine their implementation phase. If the 2023 statement is not available, 2022 will be used.
