# Requirements

## 1. Automation & Intelligence
- **R1.1: Expense Extraction**: Automatically parse receipt data (amount, vendor, tax, date) with >90% accuracy.
- **R1.2: PO Extraction**: Prefill Purchase Orders from uploaded PDFs/Images.
- **R1.3: Inventory Alerts**: Auto-generate notifications for low stock.

## 2. Beginner Friendly UX
- **R2.1: Guided Onboarding**: Step-by-step wizard for first-time merchant setup.
- **R2.2: Simplified Accounting**: Use plain language for financial terms (e.g., "Money In" vs "Credit").
- **R2.3: Dashboard Insights**: Visual charts that highlight immediate action items.

## 3. Financial & Legal Compliance
- **R3.1: SST Reporting**: Accurate generation of SST-02 forms based on journal entries.
- **R3.2: E-Invoicing**: Full integration with LHDN MyInvois for outgoing invoices.
- **R3.3: POS Integrity**: Daily session reconciliation to prevent cash discrepancies.

## 4. Platform & Scale
- **R4.1: Marketplace Sync**: Real-time inventory and order sync with Shopee/Lazada/TikTok.
- **R4.2: Delivery Multi-courier**: Integration with Lalamove and potential future providers.
- **R4.3: Secure Tenancy**: Zero data leakage between merchant partitions.
